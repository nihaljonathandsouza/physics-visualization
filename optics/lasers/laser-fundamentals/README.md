# Laser Fundamentals

A Manim-based visualization explaining the fundamental processes involved in laser operation, from light–matter interaction to population inversion and optical amplification.

## Concepts Visualized

- Absorption
- Spontaneous emission
- Stimulated emission
- Population inversion
- Excited and ground states
- Optical amplification
- Optical cavity
- Laser feedback
- Coherent photon generation

## Overview

The animation is structured as a sequence of three stages.

The first stage introduces the interaction between light and matter, showing absorption, spontaneous emission, and stimulated emission.

The second stage introduces population inversion and illustrates how the population of excited states must be increased relative to the lower-energy state for stimulated emission to dominate.

The final stage introduces the optical cavity and shows how repeated interaction of photons with the gain medium provides optical amplification and establishes the basis for laser operation.

## Teaching Focus

The visualization presents laser operation as a sequence of physical processes rather than treating the laser as a single black-box device.

It connects:

**Light–Matter Interaction → Population Inversion → Optical Cavity → Laser Operation**

This provides a visual framework for introducing the basic physics behind laser generation.

## Implementation

**Software:** Python, Manim Community Edition

The animation was created programmatically using particles, energy-level representations, photon paths, optical components, mathematical labels, and animated transformations.
