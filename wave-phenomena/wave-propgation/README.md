# Advanced Wave Propagation

A Manim-based visualization following the propagation of light through multiple wave and optical phenomena.

## Concepts Visualized

- Spherical wavefronts
- Plane-wave approximation
- Diffraction
- Huygens' principle
- Reflection
- Refraction
- Wave propagation through a transparent medium
- Change in wavelength and propagation speed
- Lateral displacement

## Overview

The animation follows a propagating wavefront through a sequence of physical situations.

It begins with spherical wave propagation and develops the plane-wave approximation before introducing diffraction through a narrow opening.

The propagating wave then encounters a reflecting surface before entering a transparent medium, where the change in propagation behaviour is visualized.

The animation combines several wave and geometrical-optics concepts into a continuous propagation sequence.

## Teaching Focus

The visualization is designed to connect several concepts that are often taught separately by following a wavefront through different physical situations.

Rather than presenting isolated diagrams, the animation shows how the wavefront evolves as its environment changes.

## Implementation

**Software:** Python, Manim Community Edition

The scene uses custom mathematical functions for wavefront construction and propagation, together with dynamic geometric objects, transformations, and camera movement.
