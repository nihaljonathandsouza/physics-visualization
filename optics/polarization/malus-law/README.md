# Linear Polarization & Malus's Law

A Manim-based visualization introducing electromagnetic wave polarization and the intensity variation described by Malus's law.

## Concepts Visualized

- Electromagnetic waves
- Electric and magnetic field components
- Linear polarization
- Polarizer and analyzer
- Transmission axis
- Polarization angle
- Malus's law
- Variation of transmitted intensity

## Overview

The animation begins by visualizing the electric and magnetic field components of an electromagnetic wave.

It then introduces linear polarization by focusing on the electric field component and shows how a polarizer restricts the allowed direction of the electric field.

A second polarizer (analyzer) is introduced with a variable angle between the transmission axes. The animation demonstrates how changing this angle affects the transmitted electric field and the resulting intensity.

## Mathematics

For linearly polarized light passing through an analyzer at an angle θ, the transmitted intensity follows Malus's law:

$$
I = I_0 \cos^2\theta
$$

where:

- $I_0$ is the intensity before the analyzer
- $I$ is the transmitted intensity
- $\theta$ is the angle between the transmission axes

The animation visualizes the relationship between the polarization angle and transmitted intensity.

## Teaching Focus

The visualization is designed to build an intuitive connection between the direction of the electric field, polarization, the orientation of a polarizer, and the resulting change in transmitted intensity.

## Implementation

**Software:** Python, Manim Community Edition, NumPy

The animation was created programmatically using vector-based electromagnetic field representations, geometric constructions, mathematical expressions, and animated changes in polarization angle.
