# Vector Geometry

A Manim-based visualization of vector geometry, covering vector components, magnitude, dot products, projections, orthogonality, and the cross product in three dimensions.

## Concepts Visualized

- Vector magnitude and direction
- Vector components
- Geometric decomposition
- Dot product
- Projection of one vector onto another
- Orthogonality
- Zero dot product
- Cross product
- Three-dimensional vector geometry

## Mathematics

For a vector

$$
\vec A = A_x\hat{i}+A_y\hat{j},
$$

its magnitude is

$$
|\vec A|=\sqrt{A_x^2+A_y^2}.
$$

The dot product between two vectors is

$$
\vec A\cdot\vec B
=
|\vec A||\vec B|\cos\theta.
$$

Geometrically, the dot product is related to the projection of one vector onto another.

For perpendicular vectors,
$$
\theta=90^\circ
$$

and therefore
$$
\vec A\cdot\vec B=0.
$$

The cross product is visualized in three dimensions as

$$
\vec A\times\vec B,
$$

producing a vector perpendicular to both input vectors.

## Optics Connection

Vector methods are fundamental to describing electromagnetic fields, propagation directions, surface normals, projections, and forces in optical and electromagnetic systems.
