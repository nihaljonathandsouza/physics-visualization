# Myopia & Optical Correction

A Manim-based visualization of myopia and its optical correction using a concave corrective lens.

## Overview

This animation illustrates how excessive refractive power causes incoming parallel rays to focus in front of the retina in a myopic eye. It then introduces a concave corrective lens and visualizes how the diverging lens changes the ray geometry so that the rays focus on the retina.

## Concepts Visualized

* Schematic model of the eye
* Crystalline lens and retina
* Focusing of parallel rays in a myopic eye
* Concave corrective lens
* Divergence introduced by the corrective lens
* Formation of the corrected focal point on the retina

## Implementation

**Software:** Python, Manim Community Edition v0.18.1

The animation was constructed programmatically using Manim's geometric primitives, vector-based ray construction, transformations, and animation choreography.

## Note

This is a conceptual physics visualization and is intended to illustrate the optical principles of myopia correction rather than serve as a clinical eye model.
